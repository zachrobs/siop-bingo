HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to Inaugural SIOP 2026 Bingo.

  1. 1 Choose a name for yourself and enter the password you received from the admin to access your personal board.
  2. 2 Your board has 25 squares — 24 pre-set prompts and a FREE center space, each describing something you might witness or experience at SIOP.
  3. 3 Tap a square to mark it when you witness or experience what it describes. Tap again to unmark.
  4. 4 Get BINGO by completing a full row, column, or diagonal — 5 marked squares in a line.
  5. 5 Your progress saves automatically — close the browser and come back anytime, your board will be right where you left it.
  6. 6 When you get BINGO, you'll see a notification and your name and timestamp will appear on the leaderboard for all players to see.
  7. 7 Honor system — only mark squares you've actually witnessed. We're judgy people. Act accordingly.
  8. 8 This is not for general consumption — please use your best discretion about who you share this with. When in doubt, don't.
